LYMI by NOBLESS

AI agents, automations and digital products—discover, test, build and sell with LYMI.

LYMI is the first public product from NOBLESS. Its live platform brings together AI agents, automations, apps, digital services and a builder studio.

The platform

One place for customer-facing AI and automation.

LYMI is being built to help teams handle conversations, capture opportunities and automate repeatable work—without losing control of the customer experience.

01 / Respond

Missed Call Hunter

Follow up on missed calls and help turn unanswered enquiries into conversations your team can continue.

02 / Converse

Voice Agent

Give customers a natural voice interface for common questions, routing and service workflows.

03 / Connect

Omni Inbox

Bring customer conversations into a clearer workspace so teams can follow context and respond consistently.

LYMI Studio

Build, test, deploy and sell your own AI.

Start from a sentence, assemble a visual workflow or use developer mode. LYMI Studio brings the build cycle into one workspace.

Create & configure

Choose what to build, then define its instructions, tools and data.

Test & debug

Run your project, inspect live logs and follow each step to understand what happened.

Deploy & publish

Save and switch on your project, then publish it to the LYMI Marketplace.

Monetize

Set a price and license for your published work. LYMI states that creators earn 90% of every sale.

Developer mode lets you edit the project as JSON, using the same engine with more direct control.

Marketplace

Discover. Test. Buy. Build. Sell.

Explore AI agents, automations, apps, prompts, creative products, data, education and professional services. Try eligible free demos before buying, with clear product pages and pricing.

The marketplace includes both ready-to-use digital products and services delivered by the LYMI team or sellers.

Built with responsibility

Useful automation. Human control.

Clear hand-off

When an agent cannot confidently help, the intended experience is to say so and route the request to a person.

Connected by choice

External services should be connected deliberately, with clear permissions and account-level configuration.

Honest product status

LYMI is being developed iteratively. Specific integrations and capabilities should be checked in the live product before relying on them.

Customer agents

From the first ring to the next customer.

LYMI's current website demonstrates a missed-call journey: detect an unanswered call, send a quick message, qualify the request, route urgent cases to a person and help the team follow up or book.

Missed-call recovery

Follow up with callers who could not reach your business and collect the details your team needs.

Voice & messaging

Offer customer conversations through voice and messaging experiences, including WhatsApp and website chat.

Human hand-off

When the agent does not know, it should not guess. It collects the details and hands the conversation to your team.

Languages & business model

Built for conversations across languages.

LYMI presents English, French, Arabic and Moroccan Darija support for customer conversations.

Offers vary: some products are free to try, some are one-time purchases or subscriptions, and selected services use a pay-per-result model. Check the individual listing for its exact price and terms.
