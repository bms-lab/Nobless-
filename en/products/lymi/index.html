LYMI by NOBLESS

AI agents that help businesses respond, serve and move work forward.

LYMI is the first public product from NOBLESS. It brings customer-facing AI agents, workflow automation and a marketplace into one evolving platform.

Explore LYMI ↗Talk to NOBLESS
The platform

One place for customer-facing AI and automation.

LYMI is being built to help teams handle conversations, capture opportunities and automate repeatable work—without losing control of the customer experience.

01 / Respond

Missed Call Hunter

Follow up on missed calls and help turn unanswered enquiries into conversations your team can continue.

02 / Converse

Voice Agent

Give customers a natural voice interface for common questions, routing and service workflows.

03 / Connect

Omni Inbox

Bring customer conversations into a clearer workspace so teams can follow context and respond consistently.

LYMI Studio

Build an agent around the way your business works.

The Studio direction combines guided no-code setup with a more advanced workspace for teams that need deeper control.

Start with a template

Choose a starting point for a salon, clinic, restaurant, home service or online store, then adapt the conversation and workflow to your operation.

Design the workflow

Connect steps such as receiving a message, answering a question, collecting details, booking an appointment or handing the conversation to a person.

Connect your tools

The product direction includes connections to messaging, calendars and payment services. Availability depends on the integration and account configuration.

Test before publishing

Preview an agent, check its responses and review the hand-off path before making it available to customers.

Marketplace

Discover, test and sell AI capabilities.

LYMI is designed to bring together ready-to-use agents, automations and digital services. Users can explore solutions; builders can package and offer their work through a shared marketplace model.

Marketplace availability, seller onboarding and transaction features may evolve as the platform develops.

Channels & connections

Meet customers where they already are.

LYMI's website lists WhatsApp Business, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Shopify, phone, Google Calendar, HubSpot, Salesforce, payments, email and website chat among its intended connections.

The availability of each connection depends on the current product release, provider permissions and account setup. Confirm the specific integration in LYMI before relying on it.

The commercial model

Designed around outcomes, not just seats.

LYMI presents a results-based offer for selected customer agents: recovered calls, booked appointments or answered customers. The published promise is “no result, no bill” for eligible result-based services.

Pricing, eligibility and measurement depend on the selected agent and offer. Review the current terms on lymi.io before purchasing.
